2Account Types

Lvlup Futures offers two account structures:

Lvlup Accounts

Flexible evaluations with Monthly or One-Time payment options. After passing, the trader completes KYC and pays the required activation fee before the funded account becomes active.

Starter Accounts

One-Time Fee evaluations with no monthly subscription and no activation fee after passing.

Billing & Payments

  • The monthly payment applies only while the evaluation remains active.

    Billing stops when the evaluation is passed, breached or cancelled. It does not continue as a monthly funded-account subscription.

  • The trader pays once for access to the Lvlup evaluation instead of using a recurring monthly subscription.

    After passing, the required activation fee must still be paid before the funded account becomes active.

  • Starter Accounts are available through a One-Time Fee only.

    There is no monthly Starter subscription and no separate activation fee after passing.

  • Lvlup Accounts require an activation fee after the evaluation is passed.

    Starter Accounts do not have an activation fee.

    The applicable Lvlup activation amount is shown in the external dashboard before payment.

  • No ongoing monthly evaluation charge applies after a Lvlup Account is passed and activated.

    Starter Accounts also have no funded monthly subscription.

  • Yes. An active monthly Lvlup evaluation can be cancelled through the dashboard.

    You will retain access until the end of the current paid billing period, and no further monthly renewal will be charged.

  • A One-Time evaluation does not have a recurring subscription to cancel.

    The account remains available according to its evaluation lifecycle unless it expires, becomes inactive or is breached.

  • Available promotional or affiliate codes can be entered inside the external dashboard.

    The dashboard will show the adjusted price before payment. Starter Account discounts cannot exceed 10%.

  • Discount-code stacking depends on the promotion and dashboard rules active at checkout.

    The dashboard will automatically apply only the discounts that are eligible for the selected account.

  • Available payment methods are displayed in the external dashboard during checkout.

    Payment availability can vary based on location, account type and current payment-provider support.

  • Refund eligibility depends on the account status, payment method and applicable purchase terms.

    Once trading activity has started, the evaluation is generally not eligible for a standard refund. Contact support before placing a trade if you believe a payment was made incorrectly.

Verification & Payouts

  • KYC means Know Your Customer. It is an identity-verification process used to confirm that an account belongs to the registered trader.

    KYC helps protect accounts, payouts and the integrity of the program.

  • Lvlup Futures uses Sumsub for identity verification.

  • KYC is normally completed after passing the evaluation and before funded-account activation or payout access.

    Additional verification may be requested when required for account security or compliance.

  • Sumsub may request a government-issued identity document and additional verification information.

    The exact requirements depend on the trader's country and the verification checks required for the account.

  • Approved payouts are currently supported through:

    • Rise
    • Crypto

    Available options may depend on location and compliance eligibility.

  • Approved Lvlup payout requests are generally processed within 24 to 48 hours after review.

    This processing period is separate from the account's payout-eligibility schedule.

  • No. Payouts must be made to the verified account holder through an approved payout method.

    Account ownership, KYC information and payout details must belong to the same person.

  • A request may be reviewed for identity verification, account-rule compliance, trading activity, payout eligibility and payment-provider requirements.

    Additional review helps ensure that the payout is sent securely to the correct trader.

  • Payout Protector can protect eligible funded gains if a covered funded Lvlup Account experiences a qualifying hard breach.

    The account still closes after the breach, but eligible gains may remain payable under the applicable conditions.

  • Payout Protector adds 25% to the selected eligible account price.

  • No. It is an optional enhancement.

    An account can be purchased and traded without adding Payout Protector.

  • Payout Protector must be selected during the eligible account-purchase process.

    It cannot be added after the account has already experienced a breach.

  • No. Payout Protector does not increase the drawdown limit, remove trading conditions or keep a breached account active.

    It only affects the treatment of eligible funded gains after a qualifying breach.

  • Availability is shown in the external dashboard during account configuration.

    Only account types and purchases marked as eligible should display the Payout Protector option.

Account Breach

  • A breach closes the affected account and prevents further trading activity through it.

    The dashboard will display the account status after the breach has been recorded.

  • No. Breached accounts cannot be reset, restored or reopened.

    A trader who wants to continue must purchase a new evaluation.

  • No. A breached account cannot be reactivated by paying an additional fee.

    A new evaluation must be started.

  • Monthly evaluation billing stops after the account has been breached.

    The breached account cannot be used for the remaining billing period.

  • Yes, provided your account remains eligible and you continue to comply with the program's account and allocation requirements.

  • Eligible gains in a breached funded Lvlup Account may be protected when valid Payout Protector coverage applies.

    Without applicable protection, remaining gains may become ineligible under the funded-account terms.

Lvlup Elite

  • Lvlup Elite is a private recognition tier for traders who demonstrate verified performance over time.

    It is designed to offer recognition, member credentials, events, experiences and rewards beyond the standard funded journey.

  • No. Elite membership cannot be purchased through a fee or subscription.

    It must be earned through verified trading achievements.

  • A trader qualifies by accumulating 10,000 verified Elite points.

    The 10,000 entry points are redeemed when membership begins. Any points above the entry requirement remain in the member's balance.

  • Elite points can be earned through:

    • 1 point per $1 paid out by Lvlup Futures
    • 0.1 points per $1 from an approved external futures payout
    • 200 points for a passed Lvlup Account
    • 50 points for a verified passed account at another futures firm

    External achievements are subject to verification.

  • Elite membership remains active for 12 months from the member's activation date.

    Members must requalify through verified performance to maintain membership for another annual period.

  • Benefits can include:

    • Digital and physical Elite member cards
    • Private member events
    • Exclusive trips and experiences
    • Profile recognition
    • Achievement credentials
    • Access to the Elite progression system
    • The ability to use points toward eligible experiences

  • For eligible Elite events and experiences:

    1 Elite Point = $1

    Points may be used to cover an experience in full or applied as a partial discount.

  • The 10,000 points required for entry are redeemed when membership begins.

    Any verified balance above 10,000 remains available in the member's account.

  • Yes. Approved payouts from other futures prop firms can contribute Elite points after valid proof has been submitted and approved.

Affiliate Program

  • Approved affiliates receive a unique referral link or discount code.

    When an eligible customer completes a qualifying purchase using that referral, the commission is recorded in the affiliate dashboard.

  • Complete the Affiliate Application with your personal or business information, audience details and intended promotional methods.

    Applications are reviewed before affiliate access and codes are activated.

  • Approved affiliates earn 10% commission on eligible:

    • Monthly evaluation payments
    • One-Time evaluation payments
    • Activation fees

    Commission is tracked through the affiliate dashboard.

  • Affiliate payouts are available every 14 days after the applicable verification and approval process.

  • Affiliate payouts are supported through:

    • Crypto
    • Rise

    KYC must be completed before the first payout can be processed.

  • No minimum affiliate payout amount currently applies.

  • Lvlup Futures does not charge an internal affiliate payout fee.

    External payment providers may apply their own network or processing costs.

  • Yes. Affiliates can request a preferred custom code during the application or approval process.

    All codes remain subject to availability, review and activation.

  • The affiliate dashboard can display:

    • Referral activity
    • Qualifying purchases
    • Commission earned
    • Payout history
    • Available commission balance
    • Affiliate rewards and progress

  • Path to Retainer is a performance-based affiliate progression opportunity.

    An affiliate who generates at least $3,000 in commission for two consecutive months may qualify for a fixed $5,000 monthly retainer, paid in addition to ongoing commission earnings.

    Eligibility remains subject to performance review and the applicable agreement.

  • Affiliate performance is reviewed every 14 days.

    Reviews may consider referral quality, sales performance, compliance, promotional conduct and continued program eligibility.
