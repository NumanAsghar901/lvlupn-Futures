Rules You Should Know

One Structure Across Every Evaluation

The account size changes. The core trading framework remains consistent.

01

No Daily Loss Limit

Trade without a separate daily loss limit while staying within your maximum drawdown.

02

40% Consistency Rule

Your most profitable trading day cannot exceed 40% of your total profits.

03

Payouts Every 14 Days

Engine Funded Traders can request payouts every 14 days.

04

10% Payout Cap

Industries Biggest

Withdraw up to 10% of the account size per eligible payout.

05

EOD Trailing Drawdown

Your maximum drawdown trails based on your end-of-day account balance.

06

Hold Through News

Trade through scheduled economic news without a news trading restriction.
